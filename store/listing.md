## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Tambola (also called Housie) runs on one phone and calls the numbers so nobody has to.

Put the phone in the middle of the room, press start, and it draws numbers 1–90 at random
and reads each one out loud — with the traditional caller rhymes, if you want them. The
board on screen shows everything called so far, so a disputed claim takes one glance to
settle.

WHAT IT DOES

• Calls numbers 1–90 out loud using your phone's built-in voice
• Traditional Housie rhymes for every number — toggle them off for plain calling
• Full 1–90 board, colour-coded, with the last call ringed
• Auto-call at 5, 7 or 10 seconds — or step through manually at your own pace
• Undo the last call when someone talks over it
• Prize tracker for Early Five, Top Row, Middle Row, Bottom Row and Full House — tap to mark claimed
• Called / remaining counters and a game timer
• Light and dark themes, plus automatic

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY

No sign-in. No account. No ads. No in-app purchases. No permissions beyond what Android
requires to run the app. It works with the phone in aeroplane mode, so a weak signal at a
family function or a housing-society party is not your problem.

Nothing you do in the app leaves your phone. There is no analytics, no tracking and no
data collection of any kind.

WHO IT IS FOR

Anyone who ends up being the caller — at Diwali and Christmas gatherings, kitty parties,
office parties, society events, weddings, cruise nights, or a quiet evening at home. It
is the fairest way to draw: the phone has no favourites and never loses a ticket.

HOW TO PLAY TAMBOLA

Every player has a ticket of 15 numbers spread over three rows. The caller draws numbers
one at a time; players cross off matches. Prizes go to the first to complete Early Five
(any five numbers), a full Top, Middle or Bottom row, and finally Full House — all fifteen.
This app is the caller. Bring your own tickets.
```
